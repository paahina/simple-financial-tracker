SIMPLE FINANCIAL TRACKER
Business Rules
===============

1. ACCOUNT BALANCE

For an account:

balance =
initialBalance
+ income
- expenses
+ transfersIn
- transfersOut
+ debtPaymentsReceived

The exact treatment of debt payments in income reporting is separate from account balance calculation.

2. TOTAL USER MONEY

Total money is the sum of active account balances.

Outstanding debts are NOT part of current cash/account balance.

Example:
Accounts = PHP 40,000
Others owe user = PHP 5,000

Current money = PHP 40,000
Receivables = PHP 5,000
Potential total resources = PHP 45,000

The dashboard should keep these concepts distinct.

3. INCOME

Income increases an account.

Examples:
- Salary
- Freelance payment
- Business income
- Interest
- Gift

4. EXPENSE

Expense decreases an account.

Examples:
- Food
- Transportation
- Bills
- Shopping

5. TRANSFER

Transfer moves money between the user's own accounts.

Example:
BPI PHP 20,000 -> GCash PHP 2,000

After:
BPI PHP 18,000
GCash increases by PHP 2,000

Total account money is unchanged.

6. DEBT

A debt is money another person owes the user.

originalAmount is fixed after creation except through an explicit edit operation.

remainingAmount is derived from:
originalAmount - sum(payments)

7. DEBT PAYMENT

A payment reduces the debt.

Example:
Debt = PHP 5,000
Payment = PHP 2,000
Remaining = PHP 3,000

The selected receiving account increases by PHP 2,000.

8. REPORTING

Income and expense reports use normal transactions.

Transfers are excluded.

Debt balances are shown separately.

Debt payments should be shown separately unless the reporting design explicitly classifies them as income.

9. DATES

Use a transaction/payment date for when the financial event occurred.

Use createdAt/updatedAt for record lifecycle metadata.

Do not substitute createdAt for transactionDate in financial reporting.

10. DELETION

Editing/deleting financial records must update derived reports.

Where possible, prefer cancellation/soft deletion for important financial history rather than irreversible destruction.

11. CURRENCY

MVP can default to PHP.

Store currency explicitly on accounts so future multi-currency support remains possible.

Do not implement currency conversion in MVP.

12. USER OWNERSHIP

Every account, transaction, transfer, category, debt, and debt payment belongs to a user.

The server derives ownership from authentication.

13. CATEGORIES

Expense transactions use expense categories.

Income transactions use income categories.

Transfers do not require a normal income/expense category.
